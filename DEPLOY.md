# 🚀 Deploy Anonymous AI — the easy phone-only way

No terminal. No code. Just your browser. ~10 minutes.

---

## ✏️ Step 1 — Rename your repo (optional but nice)

1. On your phone, open **github.com/curiousstitches/codepilot**
2. Tap **Settings** (top of the repo)
3. Under **Repository name**, change it to **`anonymous-ai`**
4. Tap **Rename**

> Your old link will auto-forward, so nothing breaks.

---

## 📦 Step 2 — Save the old CodePilot (so nothing's lost)

1. In the repo, tap **Add file → Create new file**
2. In the name box type: `codepilot-archive/README.md`
3. Type a line like `Old CodePilot project, archived.`
4. Tap **Commit changes**

(That makes the archive folder. The full old code is also in the `codepilot-archive.zip` Anonymous AI gave you — keep that safe.)

---

## ⬆️ Step 3 — Upload the new app

1. In the repo, tap **Add file → Upload files**
2. Upload **`index.html`** (the app) and **`README.md`** (the new readme)
3. Tap **Commit changes**

> The app MUST be named `index.html` — that's the magic name GitHub looks for.

---

## 🌐 Step 4 — Turn on the live website

1. Repo → **Settings → Pages**
2. Under **Source**, pick **Deploy from a branch**
3. Branch: **main**, folder: **/ (root)** → **Save**
4. Wait ~2 minutes, refresh the page

🎉 Your live link appears: **https://curiousstitches.github.io/anonymous-ai/**

---

## 📲 Step 5 — Make it an app

1. Open that live link in **Chrome**
2. Menu (⋮) → **Add to Home Screen**
3. Launch from the new icon → fullscreen, no browser bars

From now on:
- ✅ The in-app **Update** button works automatically
- ✅ No more re-downloading files
- ✅ Share the link with anyone — it just works

---

## 🆙 Later: make it a real installable APK (still no code)

1. Go to **pwabuilder.com**
2. Paste your live link
3. Tap **Package for stores → Android**
4. Download the APK, install it like any app

That's it. You built and shipped a real app — from your phone. 🕶️
